About Us

A Comfortable Stay in the Heart of Harar

Wonderland Hotel sits in front of Harar get in Harar, one of Ethiopia's most storied cities — a place of walled old streets, coffee tradition and centuries of history. We aim to offer guests a comfortable base for exploring the city, with attentive, welcoming service throughout their stay.

Whether you are visiting for business, culture or simply passing through, our team is on hand to help make your time in Harar easier and more enjoyable.
